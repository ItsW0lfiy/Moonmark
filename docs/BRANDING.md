# Branding

`assets/branding/moonmark-logo-source.png` is the approved, untouched source. `moonmark-logo.png`, `moonmark-symbol.png`, PNG icon sizes, and `moonmark.ico` are derived native assets. The compact symbol appears in the title bar and window icon, and Cargo's Windows resource step embeds `moonmark.ico` in the executable. The full mark is reserved for the empty state and documentation.

Do not redraw, trace, recolor, stretch, or replace the approved mark. Moonmark is independent and must not inherit branding, identifiers, or assets from another application.
