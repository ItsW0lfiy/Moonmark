# Layout transitions

This paragraph represents the final line of a chapter. The thematic break below
should remain close enough to read as a deliberate section transition.

---

## Heading after a thematic break

The heading should have normal section spacing, without a wrapped divider or an
invisible empty paragraph between these elements.

![Image immediately after text](images/image-with-spaces.png)

## Heading immediately after an image

The image and this heading should use ordinary document spacing.
